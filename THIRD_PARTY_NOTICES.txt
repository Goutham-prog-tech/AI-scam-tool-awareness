Third-party components bundled with this project
- Tesseract.js and tesseract.js-core (Apache-2.0): in-browser image-to-text (OCR) engine, static/ocr/
- Tesseract trained language data for English, Telugu and Hindi (Apache-2.0): ocr_lang/
- Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Noto Sans Telugu and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1), via Fontsource: static/fonts/
